Operator-side advisory  ·  Performance and scale-up

Running a Controlled Environment Agriculture (CEA) facility according to plan is harder than anyone tells you before you're in it. The gap between where your operation is and where it needs to be — on costs, yield, quality, or all three — is real, but the solutions are not always obvious. And if you're scaling up, you're being asked to make decisions about systems, equipment, and operational models you haven't run before. I've lived both situations from the inside.

Most CEA facilities have a version of the same underlying problem: projections built before the farm has real data to work with — assumed yield, estimated labor efficiency, raw materials and energy forecasts from the best available information. The models are useful for early-stage decisions, but are stale by launch day and don't age well from there. The farm keeps running, but the model stays frozen, and the gap between what the spreadsheet forecasted and how the operation actually performs is where margin disappears and operators run out of answers.

My job is to help you close the gaps and find opportunities faster.

Performance Tracking & Data Discipline
